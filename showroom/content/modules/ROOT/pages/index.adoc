= How to Use This Guide

Welcome to the *CertChain Blockchain Credentialing* demo on Red Hat OpenShift.

This lab guide walks you through a complete blockchain credentialing scenario — from issuing tamper-proof academic certificates on Hyperledger Fabric to verifying them through a public portal, and exploring the platform management capabilities underneath.

image::00-overview.png[Overview,width=100%]

== Suggested Demo Paths

*30-Minute Path (Business Focus):*

. xref:01-overview.adoc[Business Context] — Why blockchain for credentials
. xref:02-admin-issue.adoc[Issue Certificates] — Issue certs from TechPulse
. xref:03-employer-verify.adoc[Verify a Certificate] — Employer verification flow
. xref:04-student-portal.adoc[Student Transcript] — Login and view private data

*60-Minute Path (Full Platform):*

. All of the above, plus:
. xref:05-security-identity.adoc[Security & Identity] — Keycloak brokering, RBAC, MSP
. xref:06-monitoring.adoc[Monitoring] — ArgoCD and Grafana dashboards
. xref:07-scalability.adoc[Scalability] — Adding orgs, horizontal scaling
. xref:08-resilience.adoc[Resilience] — BFT consensus, fault tolerance demos
. xref:09-api.adoc[API Walkthrough] — curl commands from terminal
